Inside waits the Dragon Scroll: limitless power, for one chosen hero, the Dragon Warrior.
Master Shifu, a red panda, found a snow leopard cub on his steps, and raised Tai Lung as a son.
He trained him day and night, sure he was the Dragon Warrior. Every move was for his father's smile.
But the grand master, Oogway, an ancient tortoise, saw darkness in him, and refused.
Shifu didn't fight that decision.
Tai Lung tore through the valley for the scroll. Shifu couldn't bring himself to hurt him.
Oogway stopped him. Twenty years in chains, in a mountain prison, blaming his father.
He escaped, and came home for the scroll, and for an answer.
He beat his old master down, roaring that everything was to make him proud.
And Shifu finally said it: he'd always been proud. His pride had blinded him.
Tai Lung didn't stop. He took the scroll, and opened it.
It was blank. A golden surface, reflecting his own face.
That explains his rage. It doesn't excuse the harm.
He spent his life trying to make his father proud. He never had to.
