# Character bible — original channel renditions (2D rig + 3D sets)
| Character | Read in one look | Design (original) | Acting notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tai Lung** (snow leopard) | power held on a leash | tall, broad shoulders, pale grey-white fur with charcoal rosettes, long tail, amber eyes; plain dark training trousers, bare chest wrap; cub: huge eyes, oversized paws | child: eager, looks UP at Shifu after every move; adult: jaw tight, eyes searching Shifu's face even while fighting; scroll: stillness, ears drop |
| **Shifu** (red panda) | discipline covering love | small, rust-red fur, white muzzle/eyebrow marks, ringed tail, grey whiskers in the present; simple robe | younger: proud, hand on the cub's head; present: tired, hides his hands in his sleeves; on the floor: finally looks him in the eye |
| **Oogway** (tortoise) | ancient calm | very old tortoise, long neck, walking staff, few gestures | one slow head turn = the refusal |

Psychology (Tai Lung): wound = love felt conditional on achievement → belief "if I'm the best, I'm worth loving" → refusal reads as
"I'm not enough" → rage at the father who didn't defend him → twenty years turn the scroll into proof of worth → the blank scroll
removes the proof; the love he needed was never conditional. Explains, never excuses: the valley he hurt is shown.
Psychology (Shifu): pride in his student blurred into pride in himself; couldn't say love except through training; couldn't
raise his hand against his son, and couldn't raise his voice for him either.
