Daytime Emmy, Outstanding Writing in an Animated Program). Later continuity (Batman & Mr. Freeze: SubZero, 1998) reveals Nora
survived — this episode tells the story **as Victor believed it in "Heart of Ice"** ("he believed she was gone").
