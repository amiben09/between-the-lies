# Relationship map
- Circe → Glaucos: first love; she makes him a god to keep him, and so loses him.
- Glaucos → Scylla: careless desire; his gifts become Scylla's weapon against Circe.
- Circe → Scylla: envy → act of cruelty → centuries of guilt → mercy (stone).
- Object arc: the yellow flowers in the basket — gift of love (Glaucos) → weapon (pool) → the basket left empty on the
  cliff beside the stone Scylla (embellishment).
