# Character bible — ORIGINAL imagined designs (no screen version exists)
| Character | Read in one look | Design (ours) | Acting notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Circe** (titan-born witch, daughter of Helios) | lonely fire under calm | warm bronze skin, long dark-copper hair with a faint gold sheen (sun-born), amber eyes; plain undyed linen dress (no jewellery among glittering relatives); woven reed basket | young: shoulders in, eyes down, then bright with Glaucos; the pour: steady hands, wet eyes; old: straight back, grey streak, slow decisive gestures |
| **Glaucos** (fisherman → sea god) | kind → careless | mortal: sunburnt, salt-stiff curls, patched tunic, net; god: sea-green skin, hair of kelp, pearl circlet, glow | mortal: shy smile, offers a fish; god: looks past Circe |
| **Scylla** (nymph → monster) | beauty that sneers → pure hunger | nymph: pale blue-white skin, silver hair, sea-glass jewellery (his gifts); monster: six long eel-like necks with fanged heads rising from a dark mass below the waist, her own face remains at the centre, eyes still human | nymph: chin up, laughs, shows the gifts; monster: the human eyes are what we hold on; at the end the eyes close as stone climbs |
Psychology (Circe): wound = rejection by her own kind → believes the flowers reveal truth ("make them what they really are")
→ uses love-magic to keep the only kind man → loses him to the beauty she lacks → uses the same magic as revenge, calling it
truth → the monster is the truth about HER jealousy → centuries of guilt → the stone is not victory but responsibility.
After watching, the viewer will understand that the cruelty we call "revealing someone's true self" often reveals our own.
