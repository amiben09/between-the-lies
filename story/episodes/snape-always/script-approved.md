Severus Snape was a lonely kid in a coat too big for him, when he saw a girl make a flower bloom in her hand.
Lily Evans. He was the one who told her she was a witch.
At Hogwarts, she went to Gryffindor. He went to Slytherin, and drifted toward the Dark Arts.

So he made a promise. Protect the boy with her eyes.

But he kept that promise.
When Dumbledore asked if he still loved her, after all this time, he answered with one word. Always.
Dying, he asked Harry for one last thing. Look at me.
Maybe so the last thing he ever saw was her eyes.
