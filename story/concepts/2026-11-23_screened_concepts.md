## 1. The Scroll That Was Always Empty — Tai Lung & Shifu
- Source: *Kung Fu Panda* (DreamWorks, 2008 film). Character: Tai Lung (antagonist). Core: adopted son ↔ the master who raised him.
- Wound: raised to believe he'd receive the Dragon Scroll; denied it, he turned on the valley and was imprisoned for 20 years.
- Hook: "He was raised to be a hero. The day he was told no, he became the monster."
- Twist: the scroll he destroyed his life for is blank — and Shifu finally admits his love blinded him.
- Visuals: a cub's paw in a master's hand · the scroll case shut in his face · the blank golden mirror reflecting his face.
- World: "A valley of kung-fu masters waits for a sacred scroll that only one chosen warrior may read." Object: the Dragon Scroll.

## 2. The King Who Lost to a Blind Girl — Meruem & Komugi
- Source: *Hunter × Hunter*, Chimera Ant arc (manga ch. ~316-318; 2011 anime ep. 135-136). Character: Meruem (antagonist).
- Core: a king born to rule humanity ↔ a blind girl who beats him at a board game.
- Wound: born a perfect weapon with no purpose but domination; learns what a person is through losing.
- Hook: "He was born to rule humanity. He spent his last night playing a board game."
- Twist: poisoned and dying, he chooses her; she knows the poison will take her too and stays.
- Visuals: game pieces clicking in the dark · her fingers reading the board · "Komugi… are you there?"
- World: "An ant queen gave birth to a king designed to replace humanity." Object: the Gungi board.

## 3. The Sand That Was His Mother — Gaara & Yashamaru
- Source: *Naruto* (Gaara flashback, Chūnin Exam arc; mother's-sand reveal, Fourth War arc — locators to verify).
- Core: a feared child ↔ the uncle who nursed his wounds. Wound: the uncle was ordered to kill him and said he never loved him.
- Hook: "The only person who loved him was sent to kill him."
- Twist: the sand that attacked everyone was his dead mother's love, protecting him all along.
- Visuals: uncle bandaging a small hand · the kanji for "love" carved on his forehead · sand gently wrapping a child.
- World: "Ninja villages seal monsters inside children as weapons." (Reuses our Naruto village kit.) Object: bandage / sand.

## 4. He Promised Her the Fireworks — Akaza (Hakuji) & Koyuki
- Source: *Demon Slayer*, manga ch. ~154-156 (Infinity Castle). Character: Akaza (antagonist).
- Core: a branded young thief ↔ the sick girl he nursed and promised to protect. Wound: her family was poisoned while he was away.
- Hook: "He promised to protect her forever. He became the demon who forgot her name."
- Twist: the snowflake pattern of his fighting technique is her hairpin — he kept her without remembering her.
- Visuals: fireworks over a dojo · a cool cloth on her forehead · the snowflake glowing under his feet.
- ⚠️ A: the revenge massacre must be implied, never shown. World: "Demons who were once human hunt people at night."

## 5. Her Heart Belonged to Daisy — Lotso
- Source: *Toy Story 3* (Pixar, 2010). Core: a toy ↔ the girl who lost him. Wound: he walked home and found himself replaced.
- Hook: "He was the most loved toy in the world. Then he was replaced."
- Twist: Daisy only replaced him — he lied to the others that she replaced them all.
- Visuals: a rest-stop window in the rain · a new identical bear in her arms · Big Baby's pendant "My heart belongs to Daisy".
- World: "Toys are alive whenever humans aren't looking." Object: the heart pendant.

## 6. We Will Always Be Together — Gyutaro & Daki (Ume)
- Source: *Demon Slayer*, manga ch. ~95-97 (Entertainment District). Core: a hated brother ↔ the little sister he lived for.
- Wound: she was burned by a samurai she'd blinded with her hairpin; he made the deal to save her.
- Hook: "He was the ugliest boy in the district. She was the only thing he ever had."
- Twist: in death he tells her to go the other way — she jumps on his back anyway.
- Visuals: him wrapping her in his coat in the snow · the hairpin · the piggyback walk into fire (we already have piggyback).
- ⚠️ A: the burning must be implied (smoke, light), not shown.

## 7. Fly Home, Buddy — Syndrome
- Source: *The Incredibles* (Pixar, 2004). Core: a fan ↔ the hero who rejected him. Wound: "Fly home, Buddy. I work alone."
- Hook: "He wanted to be a hero's sidekick. The hero told him to go home."
- Twist: the cape — the costume of the hero he wanted to be — is what kills him.
- Visuals: homemade rocket boots · a door closing · the cape caught in a turbine (implied).
- World: "Superheroes were real, until lawsuits made them illegal." Object: the cape.

## 8. A Song for a Son Who Never Came Home — Iroh & Lu Ten
- Source: *Avatar: The Last Airbender* S2E15 "The Tales of Ba Sing Se" + Siege of Ba Sing Se backstory. Supporting character.
- Core: a conquering general ↔ the son he lost in his own war. Wound: Lu Ten died at the siege; Iroh walked away from it.
- Hook: "He spent 600 days trying to conquer a city. He lost his son there."
- Twist: the kindly tea-maker was once the war's most feared general — and the song is for a birthday.
- Visuals: a portrait with a black ribbon · a small tree on a hill · a teacup steaming.
- ⚠️ Twist 3: fans know it well; less of a reveal.

## 9. The Birthday Present — Sméagol & Déagol
- Source: Tolkien, *The Fellowship of the Ring*, Bk I ch. 2 "The Shadow of the Past". Core: two cousins fishing.
- Wound: on his birthday, Sméagol demands the ring his cousin found — and strangles him.
- Hook: "It was his birthday. He wanted one present."
- Twist: "my precious" began as "my birthday present".
- Visuals: a fishing line in a river · a gold glint in mud · a hand closing.
- ⚠️ Depth 3: little "made" backstory before the ring.

## 10. I Will Not Die a Monster — Doctor Octopus (Otto & Rosie)
- Source: *Spider-Man 2* (2004 film). Core: a scientist ↔ his wife. Wound: his fusion demo kills Rosie; the arms take over.
- Hook: "He built a small sun to help the world. It killed the woman he loved."
- Twist: he drowns his own sun — "I will not die a monster."
- Visuals: Rosie reading his poetry · the sun's light in his glasses · the reactor sinking.
- ⚠️ A: most of the film is action; the core beats are intimate.
