# Source dossier: Mr. Freeze, "Heart of Ice"
## Canon chosen
- Continuity: **Batman: The Animated Series, "Heart of Ice"** ONLY. NOT the comics, NOT Batman & Robin (1997; diamonds, MacGregor's
  syndrome name) and NOT the later "Deep Freeze" / "SubZero" (where Nora is shown alive).
- Primary source: Batman: The Animated Series, Season 1, "Heart of Ice". Aired Sept 7, 1992. Written by Paul Dini, directed by Bruce Timm.
  Mr. Freeze voiced by Michael Ansara; Ferris Boyle voiced by Mark Hamill. (Episode number differs by source: production vs air order.)
- Research method: secondary sources only (the episode itself was not watched in this session). **Watch the episode before G4.**
