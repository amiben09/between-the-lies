## Core theme check
1. Wanted to be loved for: being the man who saved Nora.
2. Taken away by: a CEO protecting his budget.
3. Became, so it never hurts again: a man who literally cannot feel warmth, who lives only for revenge.
4. Cost at the end: he fails even at revenge; alone in a cell with a toy dancer.

## Story spine
- WOUND: Boyle shuts the chamber down while Victor begs, and kicks him into the chemicals (L6-L9).
- TURN: he survives as ice and starts hunting the company (L10-L12).
- OBJECT: the snow globe dancer: intro L2, charged L3 (it looks like Nora), payoff L16.
- TWIST: the "monster" was not the only villain. A camera recorded everything, and the tape exposes the CEO (L15).
- PLANT: L8 (the small red light blinking in the corner).
- Twist breaks without the plant: YES (the tape would come from nowhere).
